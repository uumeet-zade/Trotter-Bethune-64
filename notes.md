CAMPAIGN 2064: TROTTIER / FAYYAD-BETHUNE
BRAND IDENTITY & UI/UX DESIGN SPECIFICATION
CLASSIFICATION: INTERNAL CAMPAIGN USE ONLY

CORE STRATEGY & PSYCHOLOGICAL DIRECTIVE

The objective of this digital presence is not persuasion, but domination. The user must feel that Mandy Trottier’s return to power is an inescapable reality. The design rejects empathy and approachability, utilizing brutalist typography, high-contrast imagery, and synchronized, aggressive motion to project absolute authority, technological superiority, and a demand for compliance. The campaign is fueled by grievance; the design must feel like a reckoning.

COLOR PALETTE

The palette is severely restricted to enforce discipline and focus.

PRIMARY BACKGROUND: Deep Obsidian (Hex: #0A0A0A)
Rationale: Absorbs light, creating a void-like space that feels endless and oppressive. It is darker than standard dark mode (#121212) to create maximum contrast.

PRIMARY ACCENT: Campaign Crimson (Hex: #C8102E)
Rationale: A deep, arterial red. Used sparingly but forcefully for primary directives (buttons), the party logo, and the trailing edge of light animations. It signifies danger, urgency, and power.

STRUCTURAL ELEMENTS: Cold Steel (Hex: #2A2A2A)
Rationale: Used for borders, wireframes, and secondary backgrounds. Provides harsh, industrial structure without brightening the overall layout.

TYPOGRAPHY (BASE): Stark White/Light Grey (Hex: #E0E0E0)
Rationale: High contrast against the obsidian background for brutal legibility, but slightly muted to prevent eye strain during aggressive flashes of animation.

TYPOGRAPHY

Typography acts as the primary visual weapon, relying on scale and weight rather than traditional hierarchy.

HEADLINES & SLOGANS: Anton (Google Fonts)

Style: Ultra-bold, condensed sans-serif.

Usage: ALL CAPS exclusively. Tight kerning (letter-spacing: -1px to 0px).

Rationale: Feels monolithic, unyielding, and shouted. It dominates the viewport.

BODY & UI TEXT: Space Mono (Google Fonts)

Style: Monospaced, angular, mechanical.

Usage: Standard capitalization, occasionally ALL CAPS for UI labels.

Rationale: Evokes terminal screens, military dossiers, and raw data output. Strips humanity from the messaging, making it feel like a system directive.

MOTION & ANIMATION PROFILES

Movement on the site must never feel "smooth" or "organic." It must feel mechanical, relentless, and synchronized.

THE "SWEEPING RAY" EFFECT (Primary Animation)

Mechanism: A linear-gradient background moving across the text/element via background-clip: text and background positioning.

Gradient Structure: Transparent -> Sharp White Core -> Trailing Crimson -> Transparent.

Timing: 4s linear loop (animation: sweep 4s linear infinite).

Application: Applied to the main headline ("THEY TOOK YOUR FUTURE...") and perfectly synchronized with a simulated lens flare sweeping across Trottier's sunglasses in the hero portrait.

Rationale: Creates the sensation of a scanner, a searchlight, or an unblinking, analytical gaze passing over the user. Synchronization implies total control and a highly manufactured reality.

THE TICKER

Mechanism: A continuous horizontal scroll at the bottom of the viewport.

Timing: 15s linear loop, seamless.

Content: Repetitive campaign mantras ("THE RETURN IS INEVITABLE", "NO COMPROMISE").

Rationale: Simulates breaking news alerts or emergency broadcasts. It provides a constant, subliminal drumbeat of anxiety and inevitability.

HOVER STATES ("The Snapping Effect")

Mechanism: CSS transitions set to 0s (transition: all 0s).

Application: Buttons (e.g., "DECLARE LOYALTY").

Interaction: On hover, the button background instantly flips from Crimson to Obsidian, the border turns Crimson, and the letter-spacing violently expands (letter-spacing: 6px).

Rationale: The lack of a smooth transition makes the UI feel hostile and reactive, shocking the user into action rather than gently guiding them.

IMAGERY & COMPOSITION

PORTRAITURE

Treatment: 100% grayscale with contrast boosted by 20%.

Angle: Always shot from slightly below eye level to make the subject loom over the viewer.

Expression: Stoic, unsmiling, looking slightly off-camera or obscured by tactical/reflective sunglasses.

Rationale: Eliminates warmth. The subject is framed not as a public servant, but as an icon or an architect of the new order.

OVERLAYS

Scanlines: A fixed, screen-wide overlay of faint, semi-transparent horizontal lines (background: linear-gradient... opacity: 0.3).

Rationale: Simulates viewing the site through a security monitor or compromised terminal, grounding the design in the gritty, technological reality of 2064 Caprica.

UI / UX ARCHITECTURE

BORDERS: Thick, harsh borders (2px to 4px solid #2A2A2A) box in all elements. No rounded corners (border-radius: 0). Elements are confined and controlled.

NAVIGATION: Minimized. The focus is singular. Links use intimidating language ("THE MANIFESTO", "ENFORCEMENT") rather than standard terms ("About Us", "Our Team").

THE CTA: Replaces passive requests ("Join Us") with active demands ("BET ON THE CORRECT HORSE").
